HR Interview Questions

1. Tell me about yourself.

Give a short introduction about your education, skills, projects and career goals.

2. What are your strengths?

Mention qualities such as communication, teamwork, learning ability and problem solving.

3. What are your weaknesses?

Mention a genuine weakness and explain how you are working to improve it.

4. Why should we hire you?

Explain how your skills, attitude and willingness to learn can contribute to the company.

5. Where do you see yourself in five years?

Explain your professional growth and career goals.
